A quick guide to your controls, settings and scores — plus a way to get help when you need it.

How to play

Tap to launch the ball. Move the paddle by tilting your iPhone left or right, or by dragging it with your finger. Keep the ball in play and clear the formations to advance to the next sector.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I change sound and vibration?

Tap the gear icon in the game. You can switch sound on or off and choose Off, On or Strong for vibration. Tap “Back to game” to return.

How are high scores saved?

After a completed run, use the arrows to choose your three-character name, then select “Save score”. The leaderboard is stored locally on your device. It is not an online or shared leaderboard.

Does the game need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. The current game runs on your device and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

The tilt controls feel difficult to use. What can I do?

Try dragging the paddle directly with your finger. If motion controls do not respond, close and reopen the game and check that your device is running a compatible iOS version.

I cannot hear the game.

Check that Sound is enabled in the in-game settings and that your device volume is turned up. Also check your device’s silent mode and audio output.

Will my scores move to another device?

The game does not include its own online score sync. Scores and settings are saved locally. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep this data.
