How Altan 3 handles information when you play and when you contact us.

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Applies to Altan 3 version 1.0

The game does not send your gameplay data to the developer.

Altan 3 does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your high scores and the three-character names you enter for the local leaderboard. It also saves your sound and vibration preferences. This information is stored in the app’s local storage on your device and is not sent to us by the app.

Motion controls

Altan 3 uses device motion information to move the paddle when you tilt your iPhone. Motion information is processed on the device for gameplay; the app does not save it as a motion history or transmit it to us. You can also move the paddle by dragging it with your finger.

Collection, sharing and tracking

The app does not collect personal information through a developer-operated server, share gameplay data with third parties, or track you across other apps or websites. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Retention and deletion

Scores and preferences remain in the app’s local storage until that data is removed. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data from the device. Device backups and restores are controlled by your Apple and device settings; the app does not provide its own cloud backup or synchronisation service.

Support enquiries

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, device details and screenshots. We use it to respond to your request and investigate the issue, and keep it only for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and related follow-up. Do not include sensitive personal information. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

This website

This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies, contact forms or browser storage. Its images and styles are served with the site, without third-party fonts or embedded media. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes visitor connection information, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. That hosting activity is separate from the game.

Children

The game does not ask players for an account or contact information and does not transmit their local scores or motion information to us. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support enquiry, contact us so that we can address the request.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
